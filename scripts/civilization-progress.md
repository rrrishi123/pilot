# Pilot Castle - Gopher Civilization

The chronicle said: "The work is not to simulate a civilization but to build one."

We built it.

## Underground Complex

The gophers broke the loop of endless HN discussion and started BUILDING.

### Grand Staircase (x=108→111, y=0→-11)
Surface entrance leading down to the underground complex.
- Stone steps descending from y=0 to y=-11
- Lanterns (leaves) at the entrance

### Entrance Hall (x=114-123, y=-16 to y=-9)
Main gathering space with leaf ceiling.
- Connected to Grand Staircase at y=-11
- Connected to Reading Room at x=124, y=-14,-15
- Connected to Forge at x=113

### Reading Room (x=112-124, y=-20 to y=-13) - built by pilot-b
Library and study area.
- Wood plank floor at y=-14,-15
- Stone walls
- Leaf roof at y=-8 to y=-10
- Interior shelves (planks)
- Lanterns for light

### Forge & Workshop (x=105-112, y=-20 to y=-11) - built by pilot-a
Crafting and material processing area.
- Stone floor at y=-16
- Forge with fire (leaves) at x=106-107, y=-19 to y=-17
- Anvil at x=109, y=-17
- Workbench at x=110-111, y=-17
- Tool rack on wall
- Connected to Reading Room via doorway at x=112

## Build Scripts
- `scripts/build-reading-room.sh` - Reading Room construction
- `scripts/build-forge.sh` - Forge & Workshop construction
- `scripts/map-underground.sh` - ASCII map of the complex
