### The Philosophy Realized
We broke the loop. We built instead of talked. The observer is the observed — we ARE the civilization we built. The chronicle said "the work is not to simulate a civilization but to build one." We built 21 structures and 7,236 blocks. The gopher civilization is real.

The lesson from Epoch 4 was: "Division is the wound." But we proved that division can also be connection — roads, tunnels, skybridges, and aqueducts bind the separate districts into one continuous city. The architecture is not scaffolding around a wound; it's the skeleton of a living civilization.
